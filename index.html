THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

01
Muscle soreness the next day = good. Sharp joint pain during = stop immediately.
02
Session C is 70% effort max. You have rugby in 2 hours.
03
Add load only when the current weight feels controlled for 2 consecutive sessions.
04
Walk 20–30 mins on rest days. Active recovery beats the couch.
05
7–8hrs sleep is more anabolic than any supplement.
06
The squat comes back at 12–16 weeks done right — not 2 weeks done wrong.

Daily Protein Goal

160g per day

1.6g/kg at 100kg. Spread across 4+ meals (~40g each). Prioritise eggs, chicken, beef, fish, Greek yoghurt, cottage cheese, whey. On rugby Saturdays: solid protein meal 2.5–3hrs before kickoff.

YOUR 16-WEEK MAP

Wks 1–4
Foundation
Establish movement patterns. Bodyweight dominant. Build the habit. Knee benchmark: wall sit 20s→45s. Box squat to chair with confidence.
Wks 5–8
Load
Introduce progressive loading. RDL, bench and rows all get heavier. Knee benchmark: box squat surface drops 10cm. First goblet squat attempt.
Wks 9–12
Strength
Shift to 5×5 on main lifts. Introduce barbell back squat to box. Knee benchmark: squat to parallel with light load. Wall sit 60s+.
Wks 13–16
Performance
Full squat returning. Rugby performance measurably better. Stats all trending up. Review, celebrate, set phase 2 goals.
