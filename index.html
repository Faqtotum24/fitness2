SESSION A
Tuesday · Knee Rehab & Lower Body
KNEE FIRST
This is your most important session. Patient, methodical, progressive.
Every rep is rebuilding the foundation everything else stands on.
Duration: 45–55 min
Intensity: Low–Moderate
Focus: Knee rehab, glutes, legs
Exercise Sets × Reps Rest Notes
Warmup
Supine Leg Raises
Lying flat, raise straight legs to 45°. Wakes up hip flexors and quad connection without loading knees.
2 × 15
30s
Slow and controlled. Feel the quad engage.
Knee
Wall Sit Hold
Back flat on wall, thighs parallel to floor (or as low as comfortable). This is your primary knee benchmark — record your time every session.
3 × max hold
90s
📊 RECORD your time. Target: 20s week 1 → 60s+ by week 8.
Knee
Terminal Knee Extension (TKE)
Loop a resistance band around something fixed at knee height. Stand facing anchor, slight bend in knee, press knee straight against band. Key VMO activator.
3 × 15 each leg
45s
If no band, do standing quad squeeze instead. Slow controlled extension.
Knee
Box Squat (to chair/bench)
Squat down to a surface (start at chair height ~45cm). Touch and stand. Do NOT bounce. Bodyweight only week 1. This is your squat progression benchmark.
3 × 8
90s
📊 Note depth comfort 1–10. Progress by lowering surface 2cm every 2 weeks pain-free.
Glute Bridge
Lying on back, feet flat, drive hips to ceiling. Squeeze glutes hard at top. Strong glutes protect knees directly.
3 × 15
60s
Week 3+: add barbell across hips for load.
Reverse Lunge (bodyweight)
Step BACK (not forward) — far lower load on the knee joint. Keep front shin as vertical as possible. Hold dumbbells when comfortable.
3 × 8 each leg
60s
Stop immediately if sharp knee pain. Soreness is fine, pain is not.
Calf Raise (single leg)
Hold wall for balance. Rise on one foot, lower slowly (3 seconds down). Strengthens the entire lower leg chain supporting the knee.
3 × 12 each
45s
Add weight in hand when 3×15 feels easy.
Dead Bug
Lying on back, arms up, knees at 90°. Lower opposite arm and leg toward floor without letting your back arch. Core stability essential for knee health.
3 × 8 each side
45s
Slow is the point. 3 seconds each direction.

SESSION B
Thursday · Push & Core
PUSH & CORE
Upper body pressing and core work. Shoulder-friendly from day one — no overhead pressing. We build the chest, triceps and anterior core without aggravating the shoulder.
Duration: 45–55 min
Intensity: Moderate
Focus: Chest, triceps, core
Exercise Sets × Reps Rest Notes
Warmup
Shoulder CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations)
Slow, full-range shoulder rotations — forward and back. Non-negotiable pre-session ritual for your shoulder. Takes 3 minutes and prevents issues.
2 × 5 each direction
—
Slow. Feel the full range. Never skip this.
Incline Push-Up (hands elevated)
Hands on bench or bar at chest height. Lower chest to surface, press back up. Start elevated — reduces load. This is your push-up benchmark.
3 × max (clean reps)
90s
📊 RECORD reps. Progress: lower surface as you get stronger. Goal = floor push-up.
Barbell or DB Floor Press
Lying on the floor, press bar/dumbbells up. Floor limits range of motion protecting the shoulder at the bottom — ideal for you right now. Light weight to start.
3 × 10
90s
📊 Record weight. Start conservative — 20–30kg total. Add 2.5kg when 3×12 feels easy.
Shoulder-safe
Landmine Press (or DB Neutral Press)
Bar in corner at 45° angle, press from shoulder upward in an arc. Neutral/angled pressing is much safer for your shoulder than straight overhead. Use a dumbbell if no landmine setup.
3 × 10 each arm
60s
Stop if any impingement feeling. Sub with lateral raises instead.
Dips (between chairs or bench)
Hands on bench behind you, legs extended (harder) or bent (easier). Lower until upper arms are parallel, press up. Great tricep and chest finisher.
3 × 10
60s
Keep torso upright to protect shoulder. Lean too far forward = shoulder strain.
Plank Hold
Forearm plank. Squeeze everything — glutes, abs, quads. Don't let hips sag or pike. Pure isometric core strength.
3 × max hold
60s
📊 RECORD time. Target: 30s week 1 → 2 min+ by month 3.
Pallof Press (band or cable)
Band anchored to side at chest height. Hold at chest, press straight out and return. Resists rotation — trains the anti-rotation core stability you need for rugby.
3 × 10 each side
45s
Use lightest band first. The challenge is stillness, not force.
Hollow Body Hold
Lying on back, lower back pressed into floor, arms extended overhead and legs raised ~30cm. Hold. This is the foundation of all pressing strength.
3 × 20s hold
45s
Lower back must stay in contact with floor. Regress by bending knees.

SESSION C
Saturday · Pull & Hinge · Pre-Rugby
PULL & HINGE
Rows, deadlifts, posterior chain. This session warms up exactly the muscles rugby demands — and builds the back strength that keeps your knees, shoulder and spine healthy. Keep intensity at 70% — you have rugby in 2 hours.
Duration: 40–45 min
Intensity: Moderate (70%)
Focus: Back, hamstrings, hinge
Exercise Sets × Reps Rest Notes
Warmup
Hip 90/90 Mobility + World's Greatest Stretch
Hip 90/90: sit on floor, legs in figure-4 shape, rotate side to side. World's Greatest: lunge forward, rotate trunk to sky. 5 mins total — gets everything primed for rugby.
2 × 5 each side
—
This is warmup, not exercise. Move fluidly.
Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
Hinge at hips, bar or dumbbells track down the legs, feel hamstrings load, drive hips forward to stand. This is the king of posterior chain — directly supports knee stability and rugby athleticism.
3 × 8
90s
📊 Record weight. Start 40–50kg. Add 5kg every 2 weeks. Keep back flat always.
Barbell Bent-Over Row
Hinge to 45°, row bar to lower chest. The fundamental back builder. Pairs with your floor press to balance the shoulder joint.
3 × 10
90s
📊 Record weight. Start 30–40kg. Elbows drive back, not up.
Single Arm DB Row
Knee and hand on bench. Pull dumbbell to hip, not shoulder. Unilateral work exposes and corrects side-to-side imbalances.
3 × 10 each arm
60s
Use a weight you can row with control. No jerking.
Face Pull (band)
Band at face height, pull to face with elbows high and wide. Crucial external rotation work that directly protects and rehabilitates your shoulder. Do this every session if you can.
3 × 15
45s
Light resistance. High reps. This is therapy as much as training.
Inverted Row (under bar or table)
Bar set at hip height, lie underneath, pull chest to bar with straight body. Pure bodyweight rowing. A key upper back benchmark.
3 × max clean reps
75s
📊 Record reps. Easier = raise bar. Harder = lower bar or elevate feet.
Farmer's Carry
Heavy dumbbells/plates in each hand, walk 20m, turn, return. Builds grip, core bracing and loaded carries critical for rugby contact strength.
3 × 20m
60s
📊 Record weight used. Stand tall, don't let it pull you sideways.

Nutrition
PROTEIN TARGET

Daily Protein Goal

160g per day

At 51, muscle protein synthesis is less efficient — you need more protein, not less. 1.6g per kg of bodyweight (100kg) is your floor. Spread across 4+ meals for maximum absorption (~40g per meal). Prioritise leucine-rich sources: eggs, chicken, beef, fish, Greek yoghurt, cottage cheese, whey.

On rugby Saturdays — eat a solid protein meal 2.5–3hrs before rugby (after Session C). Your muscles will need it.

Principles
YOUR RULES

THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

01
Pain vs Soreness. Muscle soreness the day after = good. Sharp, joint pain during = stop immediately. This distinction will save your knees.
02
Session C is 70% effort max. You have rugby in 2 hours. Never leave Session C feeling cooked. If you do, you went too hard.
03
Progressive overload is the game. Add weight, reps or range of motion only when the current load feels controlled and comfortable for 2 consecutive sessions.
04
Rest days are training days. Walking 20–30 mins on rest days accelerates recovery. It is not "doing nothing" — it's active recovery.
05
Sleep beats supplements. 7–8 hours is more anabolic than any product you can buy. Inconsistent sleep is your biggest recovery limiter right now.
06
The squat will come back. Trust the process. Box squats → parallel → full depth. It's a 12–16 week journey done right, not a 2-week one done wrong.
The Road Ahead
PHASE PROGRESSION

YOUR 16-WEEK MAP

Weeks 1–4
Foundation
Establish movement patterns. Bodyweight dominant. Build the habit. Knee benchmark: wall sit 20s → 45s. Box squat to chair height with confidence. No ego loading.
Weeks 5–8
Load
Introduce progressive loading on all main lifts. RDL, floor press and rows all get heavier each fortnight. Knee benchmark: box squat surface drops 10cm. First goblet squat attempt.
Weeks 9–12
Strength
Shift to 5×5 on main lifts. Introduce barbell back squat (to box). Knee benchmark: controlled squat to parallel with light load. Wall sit 60s+. Cardio capacity improving noticeably.
Weeks 13–16
Performance
Full squat pattern returning. Rugby performance measurably better. Muscle visible. Stats all trending up. Review, celebrate, and set phase 2 goals.
